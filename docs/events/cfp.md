and links for upcoming CNCF events. Use it to track deadlines for KubeCon +
CloudNativeCon and the CNCF Maintainer Summit.

## Maintainer Summit Europe 2027

The Maintainer Summit Europe 2027 takes place Sunday, March 14, 2027, at Fira
Gran Via in Barcelona, Spain. Attendance is limited to eligible maintainers and
project org members. You must also be registered for KubeCon + CloudNativeCon
Europe 2027.

:::tip CFP open

The Maintainer Summit Europe 2027 CFP closes **Tuesday, December 1, 2026, at
11:59 PM CET (UTC+1)**. TOC representatives, graduated project maintainers or
org members, TAG leads, and incubating project maintainers can submit.

:::
