# Environmental Sustainability Technical Community Group

The Environmental Sustainability Technical Community Group (TCG) focuses on advancing environmental sustainability in cloud native computing. The group was formerly TAG Environmental Sustainability and transitioned to a TCG in late 2025 (the [cncf/tag-env-sustainability](https://github.com/cncf/tag-env-sustainability) repository was archived on 18 December 2025).

## Mission

The Environmental Sustainability TCG serves as a community rallying point for discussing environmental sustainability factors in the cloud native landscape and for advocating open source projects that help observe, measure, optimize, and reduce the carbon footprint of cloud native infrastructure.

The group aims to raise awareness of environmental sustainability as a key element of open source development and to support projects that foster an understanding of energy drivers in cloud native systems.

## Focus Areas

The scope reflects the topics carried over from TAG Environmental Sustainability and the group's active initiatives on [ocgroups.dev](https://ocgroups.dev/cncf/group/env).

### Measuring Cloud Native Impact

- Defining environmental sustainability factors for the cloud native landscape
- Observing and measuring carbon footprint of cloud native infrastructure
- Promoting tooling such as [Kepler](https://github.com/sustainable-computing-io/kepler) for energy and carbon measurement

### Green Reviews

- Reviewing CNCF projects for environmental sustainability characteristics
- Continued work of the Green Reviews working group inherited from the former TAG

### Sustainability Advocacy

- Raising awareness of sustainability within the cloud native community
- Continued work of the Sustainability Advocacy working group inherited from the former TAG

### Community Events

- Organizing [Cloud Native Sustainability Month](https://tag-env-sustainability.cncf.io/), a global series of community events held annually since 2023

## Meetings

The TCG holds two public meetings per month, listed on its [Open Community Groups page](https://ocgroups.dev/cncf/group/env):

- **EMEA / APAC friendly meeting**: 2nd Tuesday, 12:00 UTC
- **AMER / EMEA friendly meeting**: 4th Tuesday, 16:00 UTC (12:00 EDT)

Meeting registration and upcoming events are available on the group's Open Community Groups page.
