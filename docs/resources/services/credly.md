---
title: Credly
description: Custom Credly badges for graduated CNCF projects
---

Credly provides custom badges for our projects (for example
[Linkerd Hero](https://www.credly.com/badges/538d249f-ec6d-4c5c-93f4-44d7c5596b36/twitter)
program).

This service is available for all Graduated projects, and is limited to **4
badges** per year.

Each year the Credly badge will "expire" (still be visible but will not be
considered active) in order to allow for Yearly badges (such as "Kubernetes
Maintainer 2025")

Submit Credly requests through the
[Credly request form](https://form.asana.com/?k=Ufi_ZE31X3EAKMTiplIEcA&d=9283783873717).
