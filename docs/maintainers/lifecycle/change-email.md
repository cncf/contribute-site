---
title: Change Email
---

Update the primary email address in your
[LFID profile](https://openprofile.dev/). CNCF uses the email address on that
profile to match your GitHub handle to CNCF services. Keep your project's public
maintainer or governance documentation current if it lists your email address.

For projects using the
[.project maintainer process](https://github.com/cncf/automation/tree/main/utilities/dot-project),
the roster records GitHub handles and teams; do not edit the generated
[CNCF .project maintainer list](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/project-maintainers-.project.csv)
to change an email address. Projects still on the
[legacy maintainer list](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/project-maintainers.csv)
should follow their existing update process.

Access to Service Desk and project mailing lists is controlled by the link
between your GitHub handle and your LFID profile. Keep your primary email
address in your LFID profile current and ensure your GitHub handle is linked
there. Existing tickets remain visible to the project, and changing your email
address adds the new address to the project.
