Hi all,

The Maintainer Summit EU CFP closes **Tuesday, Dec 1 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00)**.
Submit here:
[https://sessionize.com/maintainer-summit-eu-2027/](https://sessionize.com/maintainer-summit-eu-2027/).

<!-- truncate -->

The Summit is Sunday, Mar 14, in Barcelona, the day before KubeCon +
CloudNativeCon Europe.

It's a space for the people behind our projects to share best practices, dig
into contributing processes, and solve problems that cut across projects.

Who can submit:

- TOC Members
- Graduated Project [Maintainers](http://maintainers.cncf.io/) or Org Members
- Incubating Project [Maintainers](http://maintainers.cncf.io/)
- TAG Chairs, Technical Leads or Working Group Leads

Session categories:

- Growing Contributors
- CI/CD/Distribution
- Main Track
- Project Meetings

Notifications go out Tuesday, Jan 5. Please keep proposals free of product or vendor pitches.

Thanks,  
Nate
