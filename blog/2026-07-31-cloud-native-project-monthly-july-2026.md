## `.project` Updates

**The `.project` rollout continues to gain momentum - 152 projects** now have a `.project` repository, with more onboarding every day. Each `.project` repo gives a project a single, authoritative `maintainers.yaml` as the source of truth for its maintainers. For the full background, see [Introducing `.project` for Maintainers](https://contribute.cncf.io/blog/2026/04/22/introducing-dot-project-for-maintainers/).

Across those 152 projects we're now tracking **1,380 maintainers**. Of those, **276 don't yet have a GitHub handle connected to an LFX profile** (no matching profile data on record), so we can't reliably link them to their contributions and CNCF services.

Our project team staff are also reviewing each project's `maintainers.yaml` for accuracy against the legacy maintainers lists. A couple of things to watch for:

* **Review-and-merge PRs** - In many cases we're opening pull requests directly against a project's `/.project/maintainers.yaml`. **If your project has an open PR against `/.project/maintainers.yaml`, please review and merge it as soon as possible** - these updates are how we keep the source of truth accurate for downstream CNCF services (mailing lists, PCC access, dashboards).

* **LFX profile updates** - In other cases we'll ask maintainers to update their profile at [openprofile.dev](https://openprofile.dev) and confirm their GitHub account is linked - this is exactly what closes the gap for those 276 unconnected maintainers.

Thanks for your partnership - these quick steps keep your maintainer data accurate and trustworthy.

## Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Survey

At KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Chicago 2023, Oracle donated cloud credits to CNCF projects to use over the course of 3 years.

Since then, roughly 1 in 5 of our projects has put them to work.

[Now we're collecting use cases to highlight how these credits have helped our community. The survey takes about 4 minutes.](https://forms.gle/jYSxpNacmGHvrz439)

The form will ask about:

* What you're running on OCI (CI/CD, hosting, testing, AI workloads, etc.) and roughly what scale
* Anything you're doing on OCI that's hard to do elsewhere, or that OCI does noticeably better
* Any feedback for the Oracle team, good or bad

We're using these answers to size, scope, and shape the next phase of the partnership, so concrete details help significantly.
