Anthropic is giving CNCF project maintainers free access to Claude Ultra, its
top-tier Claude plan.

<!-- truncate -->

They've provided 3,000 codes, and each one gives one person six months of
[Claude Ultra](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan)
at no cost.

This post covers who's eligible, how the six months work, and how to request a
code.

Thank you, Anthropic, for supporting CNCF maintainers! 🎉

## Who's eligible

Codes are available to maintainers of any CNCF project at the Sandbox,
Incubating, or Graduated level.

We check eligibility against the maintainer list in your project's `.project`
repository. That's the same source we already use for other maintainer benefits.

If you haven't worked with it before, see the
[.project metadata format](https://github.com/cncf/automation/tree/main/utilities/dot-project#readme).

Anthropic also requires that you:

- Are 18 or older (the minimum age is higher in some countries)
- Are located in a
  [region where Claude is available](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)
- Accept Claude's
  [Consumer Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms) and
  [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup)

## How the six months work

- **Start:** The six months begin when you select **Redeem**, not when we send
  you the code. Redeem it when you're ready to start using it.
- **End:** If you're on the Free plan, your account returns to Free after six
  months. If you currently pay for a different Claude plan, there are a few
  extra steps. We'll send the full details with your code. See the
  [Claude Ultra FAQ](/resources/services/claude-ultra-faq).

## How to request a code

1. **Visit the Service Desk:** Navigate to
   [https://servicedesk.cncf.io/](https://servicedesk.cncf.io/).
2. **Submit a request:** Select the **Program Management** category.
3. **Provide details:** In your ticket, include your GitHub handle.

## Questions and more info

If you have questions or run into problems, comment on your Service Desk ticket
or open a new one.

Anthropic's announcement page at [claude.ai/cncf](https://claude.ai/cncf) also
lists their KubeCon+CloudNativeCon NA sessions and further reading from their
infrastructure team.
